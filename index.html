What Cessio is

Cessio helps you quit or cut back on the habits you want gone: smoking, vaping, alcohol, cannabis, gambling, social media, adult content and more. It gives you live streak counters, an SOS button for urges with guided breathing and a 10-minute timer, forgiving slip tracking, lessons and recovery timelines that link to their sources.

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.
